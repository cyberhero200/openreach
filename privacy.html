Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

OpenReach helps students find university researchers and write and send outreach emails. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. In short: your data stays in your browser and your own Google account. We do not run a server that stores it, and we never sell it.

Data you enter

Your profile, resume (text and PDF), saved prompts, selected professors, drafts and message history are stored in your browser's local storage. If you connect Gmail, a copy is also saved as a single message under a private "OpenReach" label in your own Gmail, so you can use it on other devices. Deleting that message erases the copy.

Your Gmail App Password

To send email from your account, OpenReach uses a Gmail App Password that you create and paste in. It is stored only in your browser. With each action you take, your browser sends it over HTTPS to OpenReach's own serverless functions, which connect to Gmail and then forget it. It is never written to a database or log. OpenReach uses it only to:

  • Send the emails you reviewed and approved.
  • Look for replies in your inbox, and only from the email addresses of professors you wrote to through OpenReach. No other email is read or stored.
  • Save and load your OpenReach data under the "OpenReach" label.

You can cut access at any time by deleting the App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, or by pressing "Disconnect" in OpenReach.

Third-party services your browser contacts

  • OpenAlex (api.openalex.org): your search keywords and selected universities, to find researchers.
  • The AI provider you choose (for example Google Gemini or Groq), using your own API key: your profile, resume text, saved prompt, and professor details, to write drafts and search the web. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs, to load fonts and the PDF reader.

API keys you enter are stored only in your browser and are never synced or sent anywhere except to the provider they belong to.

Your choices

  • Use OpenReach without connecting Gmail. Your data then stays only on your device.
  • Delete local data with "Delete all data on this device" in Settings.
  • Delete the App Password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords to revoke access.

Children

OpenReach is meant for students aged 13 and older. Younger students should use it only with a parent or teacher.

Contact
