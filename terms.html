Terms of use

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What OpenReach is

OpenReach is a free tool that helps students find researchers and draft emails to them. It never sends an email unless you review it and press Send.

Your responsibilities

  • Only send emails you have read and stand behind. Every email goes out from your own account, in your name.
  • Do not use OpenReach for spam, marketing, mass unsolicited email, or harassment. Write to people whose work you are genuinely interested in.
  • Check the facts in each draft. AI can make mistakes, and email addresses found online can be outdated.
  • Follow Gmail's sending limits and the rules of any AI provider you connect.

No guarantees

OpenReach is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. We are not responsible for replies or the lack of them, for emails sent from your account, or for data lost from your browser. Keep a backup with "Download backup" in Messages.

Changes

These terms may change as the app improves. Continuing to use OpenReach means you accept the current version.
